01COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What does the first paragraph say about the number of foreigners in Japan?

中第一段关于日本外国人人数的变化说明了什么？

MODEL ANSWER · 参考答案

Until the 1990s, foreigners living in Japan made up less than one percent of the population, but since then the number of people visiting, working, or studying there has increased rapidly.

中直到20世纪90年代，居住在日本的外国人还不到总人口的1%。但从那以后，赴日旅游、工作或学习的人数迅速增加。

02★ 重点复习
QUESTION · 题目

What are the reasons that foreigners come to live in Japan?

中外国人来日本居住的原因有哪些？

MODEL ANSWER · 参考答案

Reasons foreigners come to live in Japan: to work (work visas, 18%), as technical trainees (19%), and as students (22%).

中外国人来日本居住的原因包括：工作（工作签证，18%）、作为技能实习生（19%），以及作为留学生（22%）。

中在最近的法律修改之前，日本的技能工作者不被允许做什么？

07★ 重点复习
QUESTION · 题目

Why do some workers from other countries pay agents more than 1 million yen before coming to Japan?

中为什么一些外国劳动者在来日本之前，要向中介支付超过100万日元？

MODEL ANSWER · 参考答案

Because they hoped that they could work five years, pay back their debt, and save more money than they have earned in the country they came from.

中因为他们希望在日本工作五年，偿还债务，并且存下比在自己国家工作时更多的钱。

08★ 重点复习
QUESTION · 题目

What limits do international students in Japan have regarding work?

中在日本的留学生在打工方面受到哪些限制？

MODEL ANSWER · 参考答案

They are allowed to work part-time up to 28 hours a week, 8 hours a day.

中他们被允许兼职，每周最多28小时，每天最多8小时。（这里的每天8小时通常指学校规定的长期休假期间。）

10COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Why might the issues of immigrants change the Japanese view of people from other countries?

中为什么移民问题可能改变日本人对外国人的看法？

MODEL ANSWER · 参考答案

Now Japan is getting poor and poor because of shrinking its population and aging. In order to prevent Japan from falling into poverty, it is necessary to invite skilled labor forces from abroad. In fact all the rich and developed countries of the world competing for getting skilled labors. Therefore, Japan needs to improve its hospitality to foreign workers in order to compete with other developed countries.

中由于人口减少和老龄化，日本正变得越来越贫困。为了避免陷入贫困，日本有必要吸引海外技术劳动力。事实上，世界各发达国家都在争夺有技能的人才。因此，日本需要改善对外国劳动者的接纳和友好程度，才能与其他发达国家竞争。
