Unit 16 考前语法速记
  • Rarely / Little / Only + 状语 置于句首时，主句通常用部分倒装：Rarely do I… / Little did they know… / Only once have we…
  • Here comes + 名词；如果主语是代词，改为 Here it comes。
  • even / only / almost / just 尽量靠近要强调或修饰的成分。
  • Hopefully / Obviously / Unfortunately / Essentially 可作句子副词，表示说话人的评价。
