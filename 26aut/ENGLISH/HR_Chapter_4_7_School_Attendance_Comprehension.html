HUMAN RIGHTS · CHAPTER 4.7

HR4.7 · Reasons for Lack of School Attendance and Solution

阅读理解 · 失学原因与教育的作用 · Comprehension Questions

根据课堂 Answer Key 整理，部分句子稍作语法润色。617 million 为课文中的数据，不代表当前统计。

10 道问答中英对照关键词记忆

黄色：题目关键词　绿色：答案关键词

01COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Why is education important?

题目：为什么教育很重要？

ANSWER · 答案

Because education is the key to freeing people from poverty and inequality and fighting diseases.

答案：因为教育是帮助人们摆脱贫困与不平等、对抗疾病的关键。

记忆线索 education → poverty / inequality / diseases

02COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

According to the reading, how many children today do not know how to read and do mathematics?

题目：根据文章，现在有多少儿童不懂阅读和数学？

ANSWER · 答案

About 617 million children.

答案：大约6.17亿名儿童。

记忆线索 617 million = 6.17 亿（课文所述数据）

03COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What prevents children from getting an education?

题目：什么阻碍儿童接受教育？

ANSWER · 答案

Poverty is the main barrier to education.

答案：贫困是教育的主要障碍。

记忆线索 poverty → barrier to education

04COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What happens to communities where there is not enough education?

题目：在教育不足的社区，会发生什么情况？

ANSWER · 答案

A lack of education makes it more likely that children will not have access to clean drinking water and safe sewage disposal. This leads to higher rates of sickness and malnutrition.

答案：教育不足会增加儿童无法获得清洁饮用水和安全污水处理设施的可能性，进而导致更高的患病率和营养不良发生率。

记忆线索 lack of education → unsafe water / sanitation → sickness / malnutrition

05COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Why are countries responsible for the failure of children to get an education?

题目：为什么各国政府应当为儿童无法接受教育承担责任？

ANSWER · 答案

Because free primary education is a human right and should be provided to children in every country.

答案：因为免费的初等教育是一项人权，每个国家都应为儿童提供这种教育。

记忆线索 free primary education = human right

06COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What are two cultural practices that prevent children from getting an education?

题目：阻碍儿童接受教育的两种文化习俗是什么？

ANSWER · 答案

First, some children must help their families instead of attending school. Second, some girls are kept out of school because of religious beliefs.

答案：第一，有些儿童必须帮助家庭，因此不能上学。第二，一些女孩因为宗教信仰方面的原因而无法上学。

记忆线索 family duties + religious beliefs（文章中的两个例子）

07COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What barriers to education do girls face?

题目：女孩在接受教育时面临什么障碍？

ANSWER · 答案

In some African countries, such as Nigeria and Chad, young girls are often forced to marry at a very young age.

答案：在尼日利亚和乍得等一些非洲国家，女孩常常被迫在很小的年纪结婚。

记忆线索 girls → early / forced marriage

08COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

In what countries are conflicts barriers to education?

题目：在哪些国家，冲突是教育的障碍？

ANSWER · 答案

Chad and the Central African Republic.

答案：乍得和中非共和国。

记忆线索 Chad + Central African Republic

09COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

How do wars and conflicts hurt education?

题目：战争和冲突如何损害教育？

ANSWER · 答案

When children are forced to flee their homes, education becomes less of a priority. Children who remain often cannot attend school because traveling to and from school is dangerous.

答案：当儿童被迫逃离家园时，教育会变得不那么紧迫。即使没有逃离，许多儿童也因为往返学校的路途危险而无法上学。

记忆线索 flee homes / dangerous journey → missed school

10COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What are the benefits of education? Why is it important for every child to get an education?

题目：教育有什么益处？为什么每个儿童都应该接受教育？

ANSWER · 答案

Education gives people the tools to provide better for themselves and their children. It also helps them work more effectively and creates opportunities for sustainable economic growth.

答案：教育使人们拥有更好地照顾自己和子女的能力，也帮助他们更有效地工作，并为可持续经济增长创造机会。

记忆线索 support families → better work → economic growth
