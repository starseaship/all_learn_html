01
QUESTION · 题目

What are three areas of discrimination against women at work in Japan?

日本职场中，女性在哪三个方面受到歧视？

ANSWER · 答案

Women earn only about 60% of men’s salaries; less than 10% of women become managers; and pregnant women are not allowed to take leave from their jobs.

女性的工资仅约为男性的60%；成为管理者的女性不到10%；怀孕的女性无法获准休假。

关键词：60% salary → less than 10% managers → no leave（工资差距 → 晋升受限 → 无产假）

02
QUESTION · 题目

Why do women find it difficult to become managers in many Japanese companies?

为什么在许多日本公司里，女性很难成为管理者？

ANSWER · 答案

Because Japan is a patriarchal society, where men hold the power in organizations. The text says it is not because women are unqualified.

因为日本是父权制社会，男性掌握组织中的权力。文章明确指出，这并不是因为女性不具备任职资格。

关键词：patriarchal society → men hold power; NOT unqualified（父权制 → 男性掌权；不是女性能力不足）

03
QUESTION · 题目

Why do women in some companies have to quit work after their first child is born?

为什么一些公司的女性生下第一个孩子后就不得不辞职？

ANSWER · 答案

Because women are not allowed to take leave when they become pregnant, they quit after having their first child. If they work again later, it is usually part-time.

因为女性怀孕后不被允许请假，所以在生下第一个孩子后辞职。如果之后重返职场，通常也只能做兼职。

关键词：pregnant → no leave → quit → part-time（怀孕 → 不能休假 → 辞职 → 兼职）

04
QUESTION · 题目

Why is this view gradually changing?

为什么这种观念正在逐渐改变？

ANSWER · 答案

Because families find it more difficult to live on only the husband’s salary. As a result, more women enter the workforce and stay in it longer.

因为家庭越来越难以只依靠丈夫的工资生活。因此，更多女性进入劳动力市场，并且在职场中工作得更久。

关键词：one salary is not enough → more women work → stay longer（单靠丈夫收入困难 → 更多女性就业并持续工作）

05开放题 · 示例答案
QUESTION · 题目

What changes do you think the writer of this paragraph wants to see in the future?

你认为这段文字的作者希望将来出现什么变化？

ANSWER · 答案

The writer probably hopes that more women entering and staying in the workforce will lead to fairer treatment at work, including fair pay, promotion opportunities, and support for women after childbirth.

作者可能希望，随着更多女性进入职场并长期工作，女性能够在工作中受到更公平的对待，例如获得公平工资、晋升机会，以及生育后的工作支持。

关键词：more women stay employed → fairer treatment（女性持续就业 → 职场待遇改善）；公平工资、晋升与生育支持属合理推断
