01
QUESTION · 题目

What are three areas of discrimination against women at work in Japan?

日本职场中，女性在哪三个方面受到歧视？

ANSWER · 答案

The three areas are pay, promotion, and the treatment of women during pregnancy.

这三个方面是工资待遇、晋升机会，以及女性怀孕期间受到的对待。

关键词：pay → promotion → pregnancy（工资 → 升职 → 怀孕）

02
QUESTION · 题目

Why do women find it difficult to become managers in many Japanese companies?

为什么在许多日本公司里，女性很难成为管理者？

ANSWER · 答案

Because Japan has traditionally been a patriarchal society, and many companies have tended to favor men for management positions.

因为日本传统上是父权制社会，而且许多公司往往倾向于让男性担任管理职位。

关键词：patriarchal society → favor men（父权社会 → 更偏向男性）

03
QUESTION · 题目

Why do women in some companies have to quit work after their first child is born?

为什么一些公司的女性生下第一个孩子后就不得不辞职？

ANSWER · 答案

Because they are not allowed to take leave from their jobs after giving birth.

因为她们生育后无法获准休假，所以不得不辞职。

关键词：no leave → quit work（不能休假 → 被迫辞职）

04
QUESTION · 题目

Why is this view gradually changing?

为什么这种观念正在逐渐改变？

ANSWER · 答案

Because more women are entering the workforce and staying in their jobs longer.

因为越来越多的女性进入职场，并且在工作岗位上留得更久。

关键词：more women → workforce → stay longer（更多女性进入职场并长期留任）

05开放题 · 示例答案
QUESTION · 题目

What changes do you think the writer of this paragraph wants to see in the future?

你认为这段文字的作者希望将来出现什么变化？

ANSWER · 答案

The writer probably wants women to be treated equally and to have fair pay, promotion opportunities, and support to continue working after childbirth.

作者可能希望女性受到平等对待，拥有公平的薪资和晋升机会，并在生育后也能得到支持、继续工作。

关键词：equal treatment → fair pay / promotion / childcare（平等、薪资、晋升、生育后支持）
