HUMAN RIGHTS · CHAPTER 4.3

HR4.3 · Child Labor

III Comprehension Questions · 童工劳动的类型与风险

根据课堂练习照片和手写答案整理，英文答案作语法润色，并非课文原句。59% 与 7% 保留课堂数据，不代表当前统计。

8 道问答中英对照关键词记忆

黄色：题目关键词　绿色：答案关键词

01COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What are the three most common types of work?

题目：最常见的三种工作类型是什么？

ANSWER · 答案

The three most common types are agriculture, services, and industry.

答案：最常见的三种类型是农业、服务业和工业。

记忆线索 agriculture / services / industry

02COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What sector has the most significant number of child laborers?

题目：哪个行业的童工人数最多？

ANSWER · 答案

The agricultural sector (farm work) has the largest number of child laborers.

答案：农业部门（农场劳动）的童工人数最多。

记忆线索 agriculture → largest number

03COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What percentage of child laborers work in the agricultural sector?

题目：有百分之多少的童工在农业部门工作？

ANSWER · 答案

59% of child laborers work in the agricultural sector.

答案：59%的童工在农业部门工作。

记忆线索 agriculture → 59%

04COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Why is working in agriculture dangerous for children?

题目：为什么从事农业劳动对儿童来说很危险？

ANSWER · 答案

Children do difficult work that can hurt them physically. They may also handle dangerous chemicals and poisonous liquids, which can cause many health problems.

答案：儿童从事可能伤害身体的繁重劳动，还可能接触危险化学品和有毒液体，从而引发许多健康问题。

记忆线索 physical harm + dangerous chemicals → health problems

05COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What jobs are included in the service sector where child labor occurs?

题目：发生童工现象的服务业包括哪些工作？

ANSWER · 答案

These include work in hotels, restaurants, shops, and transportation companies, as well as housework.

答案：包括在酒店、餐馆、商店和运输公司工作，以及从事家务劳动。

记忆线索 hotels / restaurants / shops / transportation / housework

06COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Why is working in the service sector not necessarily safe for child laborers?

题目：为什么在服务业工作对童工来说不一定安全？

ANSWER · 答案

Children working in private homes may face physical violence or sexual abuse.

答案：在私人住宅中工作的儿童可能遭受身体暴力或性虐待。

记忆线索 housework → physical violence / sexual abuse

07COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What percentage of child labor takes place in the industrial sector?

题目：有百分之多少的童工劳动发生在工业部门？

ANSWER · 答案

7% of child labor takes place in the industrial sector.

答案：7%的童工劳动发生在工业部门。

记忆线索 industry → 7%

08COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What are some of the dangers children face when working in industry?

题目：儿童在工业部门工作时会面临哪些危险？

ANSWER · 答案

Children may work dangerously long hours for little pay and use unsafe machines.

答案：儿童可能以极低的报酬从事时间过长的危险劳动，并操作不安全的机器。

记忆线索 long hours + little pay + unsafe machines
