01CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What expectations were there for women when Tsubaki graduated from high school?

中译当椿（Tsubaki）高中毕业时，社会对女性有什么期待？

ANSWER · 答案

Women were expected to take care of their families and children and do all the housework.

中译人们认为女性应该照顾家人和孩子，并承担所有家务。

记忆线索照顾家庭＋孩子＋家务
02CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What expectations were there for women when Yuri graduated from junior college?

中译当由里（Yuri）从短期大学毕业时，社会对女性有什么期待？

ANSWER · 答案

It was quite common for women to end their careers after marriage or after having a child.

中译当时女性在结婚或生孩子之后结束职业生涯，是相当普遍的事。

记忆线索婚后／生育后离职
03CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What type of job did Yuri get?

中译由里找到了什么类型的工作？

ANSWER · 答案

She got a general staff position, which was different from a regular full-time position.

中译她得到了一份一般职（general staff）的工作，与常规全职职位不同。

记忆线索general staff ≠ regular full-time
04CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What was the economic situation in Japan when Sakura graduated from university?

中译樱（Sakura）大学毕业时，日本的经济和就业状况如何？

ANSWER · 答案

Japan was in the middle of the employment ice age, which made it difficult for graduates to find full-time positions.

中译日本正处于就业冰河期，毕业生很难找到全职工作。

记忆线索就业冰河期 → 全职难找
05CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What kind of job did Sakura get?

中译樱找到了什么样的工作？

ANSWER · 答案

She got a part-time job.

中译她找到了一份兼职工作。

记忆线索Sakura → part-time
06CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

How much more was a man's salary than a woman's when Sakura started working?

中译樱开始工作时，男性的工资比女性高多少？

ANSWER · 答案

Sakura earned only 70% as much as a man. In other words, a man's salary was about 43% higher than hers.

中译樱的收入只有男性的70%。换算过来，男性的工资比她高约43%（以她的工资为比较基准）。

记忆线索女 = 男的70%；男比女高约43%

QUESTION · 题目

Does Momoko have a full-time job or a part-time job?

中译桃子（Momoko）做的是全职工作还是兼职工作？

ANSWER · 答案

中译她有一份全职工作。

记忆线索Momoko → full-time
08CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What is an example of sexual harassment?

中译什么行为属于性骚扰？请举一个例子。

ANSWER · 答案

Inappropriate touching or making inappropriate remarks about women's bodies.

中译不适当的身体触碰，或对女性身体发表不恰当的评论。

记忆线索性骚扰＝不当触碰／身体评论
09CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What is an example of maternity harassment?

中译什么行为属于妊娠或生育相关骚扰？请举一个例子。

ANSWER · 答案

Criticizing a pregnant woman for not working as hard as others.

中译因为孕妇没有像其他人一样努力工作而批评她。

记忆线索妊娠骚扰＝因怀孕而指责
10CHAPTER 3.10
QUESTION · 题目

What is the glass ceiling?

中译什么是“玻璃天花板”？

ANSWER · 答案

The glass ceiling is an invisible barrier that prevents women from advancing to higher positions in a company.

中译“玻璃天花板”是指阻碍女性在公司晋升至更高职位的无形障碍。

记忆线索玻璃天花板＝女性晋升的无形障碍
