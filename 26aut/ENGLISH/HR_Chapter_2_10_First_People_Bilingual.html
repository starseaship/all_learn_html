01COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What did Columbus believe when he first saw people in the Americas, and where had he actually landed?

中译当哥伦布第一次在美洲看到当地人时，他以为自己到了哪里？而他实际上登陆在什么地方？

ANSWER · 答案

Columbus believed he had arrived in India and assumed the people he saw were locals. In reality, he had landed in the Caribbean.

中译哥伦布以为自己到了印度，并认为他看到的人是当地人。实际上，他登陆的是加勒比地区。

02COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

Who are “The First People”, and what European groups contributed to their mistreatment?

中译“The First People（第一批人民/原住民）”指的是谁？有哪些欧洲群体参与了对他们的虐待与压迫？

ANSWER · 答案

The First People were indigenous people of the Americas. The British, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Russians, and Spanish all contributed to their mistreatment.

中译“第一批人民”指的是美洲的原住民。英国人、法国人、荷兰人、葡萄牙人、俄罗斯人和西班牙人都参与了对他们的虐待与压迫。

03COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What factors led to the 99% population decline of the First People by the late 1800s?

中译到了 19 世纪后期，是什么因素导致第一批人民的人口下降了 99%？

ANSWER · 答案

It was caused by systematic killing through wars, murders, disease, and marginalization.

中译这是由系统性的杀害造成的，包括战争、谋杀、疾病以及边缘化。

04重点题
QUESTION · 题目

How did the Europeans’ concept of property ownership differ from the First People?

中译欧洲人的“财产所有权”观念，与第一批人民（原住民）的观念有什么不同？

ANSWER · 答案

In Europe, property ownership had long been established as a law, but for the First People, their land was communal.The Europeans stole the land and imposed their legal concept of property ownership on the people in the Americas.

中译在欧洲，财产所有权早已被确立为法律；但对第一批人民来说，土地是共有的。欧洲人夺走了土地，并把他们的法律性财产观念强加给了美洲的原住民。

05重点题
QUESTION · 题目

Why were native groups like the Cherokee forced to move, even when they followed the settlers’ rules?

中译为什么像切罗基人这样的原住民群体，即使遵守了殖民者的规则，仍然被迫迁移？

ANSWER · 答案

Despite adopting a society modeled after the United States, including courts and schools, the Cherokee were forced to relocatebecause settlers and the government desired their land.

中译尽管切罗基人建立了一个仿照美国的社会，包括法院和学校，他们仍然被迫迁移，因为殖民者和政府想要他们的土地。

06重点题
QUESTION · 题目

How did denying the First People the right to vote affect their ability to influence governance?

中译剥夺第一批人民的投票权，如何影响了他们参与并影响治理的能力？

ANSWER · 答案

Denying voting rights excluded the First People from governance,allowing European settlers to control the land without indigenous input or opposition.

中译剥夺投票权使第一批人民被排除在治理之外，让欧洲殖民者在没有原住民参与或反对的情况下控制土地。

07COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What is significant about the fact that native people in Canada were not allowed to vote until 1963?

中译加拿大的原住民直到 1963 年才被允许投票，这一事实的重要意义是什么？

ANSWER · 答案

It highlights the long-lasting denial of basic human rights to the First People, as they were not fully recognized as citizens for centuries after European arrival.

中译这凸显了对第一批人民基本人权的长期剥夺，因为在欧洲人到来后的几个世纪里，他们一直没有被充分承认为公民。

08COMPREHENSION
QUESTION · 题目

What three main human rights violations were committed against the First People in the Americas, as mentioned in the conclusion?

中译正如结论中提到的，对美洲第一批人民实施的三项主要人权侵犯是什么？

ANSWER · 答案

The denial of basic human rights continued to impact the descendants of the First People, as they were marginalized, excluded from political participation, and deprived of their property and cultural heritage.

中译对基本人权的剥夺持续影响着第一批人民的后代，因为他们被边缘化、被排除在政治参与之外，并被剥夺了财产和文化遗产。
