进入自测页面段落 / Essay重点概念

A. Paragraph / Essay Structure

段落与文章结构

Sources: Analyzing Paragraph Structure in English; Writing an Introductory / Concluding Paragraph
1
What is a topic sentence?
什么是主题句？
Answer
A topic sentence introduces the main idea of a paragraph and often tells the reader how many supporting points will follow.
中文答案：主题句介绍一个段落的主要观点，并且通常会告诉读者后面将有多少个支撑要点。
2
Where is a topic sentence located?
主题句位于哪里？
Answer
It is usually the first sentence of a paragraph.
中文答案：它通常位于段落的第一句。
3
What does a concluding sentence do? Write two things.
结尾句有哪两个作用？
Answer
It tells the reader that the paragraph is finished, and it reminds the reader of the paragraph’s main idea.
中文答案：它告诉读者段落已经结束，并提醒读者这个段落的主要观点。
4
Where is a concluding sentence located?
结尾句位于哪里？
Answer
It is the last sentence, or sometimes the last two sentences, of a paragraph.
中文答案：它位于段落的最后一句，有时是最后两句。
5
What is one thing you must not do in the concluding sentence?
在结尾句中绝对不能做什么？
Answer
You must not introduce a new idea or new information.
中文答案：不能加入新的观点或新的信息。
6
What do transition words or phrases do?
过渡词或过渡短语有什么作用？
Answer
They help the reader see the connections and relationships between ideas.
中文答案：它们帮助读者看出不同观点之间的联系和关系。
7
What do developing sections do?
发展部分有什么作用？
Answer
They explain and develop the writer’s main points in more detail.
中文答案：它们更详细地解释并展开作者的主要观点。
8
What are supporting details?
什么是支撑细节？
Answer
Supporting details are facts, examples, reasons, and explanations that support and explain the main idea.
中文答案：支撑细节是用于支持和解释主要观点的事实、例子、理由和说明。
9
What does a thesis statement do?
中心论点句有什么作用？
Answer
It expresses the main idea of the entire essay. A good thesis statement also states the writer’s position and the essay’s main points.
中文答案：它表达整篇文章的主要观点。好的中心论点句还会说明作者的立场和文章的主要要点。
10
Where is a thesis statement located?
中心论点句位于哪里？
Answer
It is usually the last sentence of the introductory paragraph.
中文答案：它通常位于引言段的最后一句。
11
What is a hook? What does the hook do?
什么是 hook？它有什么作用？
Answer
A hook is an opening sentence that interests the reader. It may be a surprising fact, an interesting question, or an imaginary situation.
中文答案：Hook 是吸引读者兴趣的开头句，可以是令人惊讶的事实、有趣的问题或假想情境。
12
What does a conclusion paragraph include? Write two things.
结论段包括哪两个部分？
Answer
It usually includes a summary statement and a final thought.
中文答案：它通常包括总结句和最后的思考。
13
What are two techniques for writing your final thought?
写最后思考的两种方法是什么？
Answer
Make a prediction or ask a question.
中文答案：作出预测，或者提出一个问题。

B. Key Concepts in Sustainable Futures and Peace & Values

可持续未来与和平价值观重点概念

Sources: Sustainable Futures 4th Edition; Peace & Values 4th Edition; Child Soldier Lecture
1
What is an ecological footprint?
什么是生态足迹？
Answer
An ecological footprint is the space we take on earth to support our life. It includes the land needed to provide resources and deal with our waste.
中文答案：生态足迹是地球为支撑我们的生活而被我们占用的空间，包括提供资源和处理废弃物所需的土地。
2
What is sustainability?
什么是可持续性？
Answer
Sustainability is the ability to continue for an unlimited number of years.
中文答案：可持续性是能够无限期持续下去的能力。
3
What are two ways that Japan spreads its ecological footprint to support over 120 million people?
日本通过哪两种方式把生态足迹扩展到其他国家，以供养超过一亿两千万人？
Answer
First, Japan imports many resources from outside its own land. Second, some of Japan’s wastes are sent overseas.
中文答案：第一，日本从国土以外进口大量资源；第二，日本的一部分废弃物被运往海外。
4
Explain the four farming methods: their characteristics, aims, equipment or techniques, advantages, and disadvantages.（说明四种农业方法的特点、目标、设备或技术、优点和缺点。）
说明四种农业方法的特点、目标、设备或技术、优点和缺点。
Answer

1. Intensive farming

Characteristics / aim: It aims for the highest amount of food production possible in an area.

Equipment / techniques: Fertilizers, chemicals that kill weeds and insects, machines for applying chemicals and harvesting, and antibiotics on factory farms.

Advantages: It produces a large amount of food and has made food cheaper.

Disadvantages: Chemicals can damage the environment; antibiotics can create super-bacteria; expensive equipment makes it difficult for small farmers to compete; intensive farming can damage the land.

2. GMO farming

Characteristics / aim: Scientists change the genes of crops in a laboratory to control insects or weeds and protect production.

Equipment / techniques: Laboratory gene editing, GMO seeds, and chemicals such as Roundup.

Advantages: Crops may kill insects or survive chemicals used to kill weeds.

Disadvantages: Weeds and insects can become resistant; farmers may have to buy seeds and more chemicals; companies control many GMO seeds; consumers have safety concerns.

3. Indoor farming

Characteristics / aim: Crops are grown inside, so production does not depend on the weather.

Equipment / techniques: Buildings, modern technology, and low-energy LED lights.

Advantages: There are no insects, so insect-killing chemicals are unnecessary; farms can be placed in cities, reducing transport time and cost.

Disadvantages: It depends on modern equipment, technology, and energy.

4. Organic farming

Characteristics / aim: It grows healthy food in a sustainable way without modern chemicals.

Equipment / techniques: Crop rotation, compost, older farming techniques, and more human labour.

Advantages: It causes less environmental damage, keeps chemicals out of food, and allows the land to continue producing food each year.

Disadvantages: It requires more labour and other costs, so organic food is more expensive to produce.

中文答案：四种方法分别是集约化农业、转基因农业、室内农业和有机农业。重点背诵每种方法的目标、使用的技术、主要优点和主要缺点。
5
What is Earth Overshoot Day?
什么是地球生态超载日？
Answer
Earth Overshoot Day is the date when humans have used a year’s worth of resources that the earth can renew in one year.
中文答案：地球生态超载日是人类用完地球一年内能够再生的全年资源的日期。
6
What are the four types of war? Give one example of each.
四种战争类型是什么？每种举一个例子。
Answer
  1. Civil war: Syria.
  2. Religious war: Sudan, where a Muslim government tried to force Islamic law on non-Muslims.
  3. War fought over territory: Russia’s 2014 invasion of Crimea, an area of Ukraine.
  4. War of terror: attacks on cities such as New York, Paris, New Delhi, and Dhaka.
中文答案：内战——叙利亚；宗教战争——苏丹；领土战争——俄罗斯于2014年入侵乌克兰的克里米亚；恐怖战争——纽约、巴黎、新德里和达卡等城市遭受的袭击。
7
What is the difference between negative peace and positive peace?
消极和平与积极和平有什么区别？
Answer
Negative peace means there is no violence, including war. Positive peace means creating conditions for people to live without suffering, with justice, equal rights, and personal well-being.
中文答案：消极和平指没有暴力，包括没有战争；积极和平指创造让人们免受痛苦、享有正义、平等权利和个人福祉的社会条件。
8
Why is no war not enough?
为什么只有“没有战争”还不够？
Answer
Because a lack of war does not mean a lack of suffering. People may still suffer from poverty, prejudice, discrimination, bullying, and other social problems.
中文答案：因为没有战争并不等于没有痛苦。人们仍可能受到贫困、偏见、歧视、霸凌和其他社会问题的伤害。
9
What does positive peace include?
积极和平包括什么？
Answer
Positive peace includes economic justice, social justice, and personal well-being. It also requires equal rights for everyone.
中文答案：积极和平包括经济正义、社会正义和个人福祉，并要求所有人享有平等权利。
10
What roles do child soldiers have?
儿童兵承担哪些角色？
Answer
They may be used as fighters, porters, cooks, messengers, or sex slaves.
中文答案：他们可能被用作战士、搬运工、厨师、传令员，或被迫成为性奴隶。
11
Why and how do children become child soldiers?
儿童为什么以及如何成为儿童兵？
Answer
Children are easier for adults to control and manipulate. Some are forced to join, while others join to escape poverty or abuse.
中文答案：儿童比成年人更容易被控制和操纵。有些儿童被强迫加入，另一些则为了逃离贫困或虐待而加入。
12
What are NGOs and NPOs?
什么是 NGO 和 NPO？
Answer
NGO means non-governmental organization. NGOs work in areas where governments cannot or do not do enough. NPO means nonprofit organization; in Japan, NPO is the legal name used for many NGOs.
中文答案：NGO 指非政府组织，在政府无法处理或做得不够的领域开展工作。NPO 指非营利组织；在日本，许多 NGO 在法律上被称为 NPO。
13
What are the two levels of overconsumption?
过度消费有哪两个层面？
Answer
At the personal level, overconsumption means buying or using things we do not really need. At the world level, it means consuming more resources than the earth can sustainably provide.
中文答案：个人层面是购买或使用并不真正需要的东西；世界层面是消耗超过地球能够可持续提供的资源。
14
Who causes the overconsumption of resources?
是谁造成了资源的过度消费？
Answer
Overconsumption is mainly caused by people in developed countries, especially the small percentage of the world’s population that uses far more than its fair share of resources.
中文答案：过度消费主要由发达国家的人造成，尤其是世界人口中使用远超公平份额资源的少数人群。
