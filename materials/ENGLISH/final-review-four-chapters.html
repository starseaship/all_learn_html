Word forms: sustainability (n.) / sustain (v.) / sustainable (adj.) / sustainably (adv.) / unsustainable (negative)

Ⅱ Structure Work

  1. Topic sentence: Japan depends on other countries for many of its needs, such as food, energy, wood, and materials for clothes.
  2. Food: domestic 39%; foreign 61%; mainly from America, China, and Australia.
  3. Energy: domestic 4%; foreign 96%; much oil and natural gas come from the Middle East.
  4. Wood: Japan supplies about 30% domestically.
  5. Wool: used to make coats and sweaters; from sheep in Australia and New Zealand.
  6. Cotton: used for towels and traditional Japanese clothes; almost half comes from the U.S.A.
  7. Conclusion: Japan should make greater efforts to supply its own needs in order to build a more sustainable future.

Sustainable Futures 2.7 · Feeding the World: Nature and Technology

Ⅰ Vocabulary Matching

1. alternative → F
2. intensive → H
3. fertilizer → A
4. harvest → J
5. afford → L
6. gene → E
7. weed(s) → B
8. efficient → C
9. eliminate → I
10. rotation → D
11. decay → G

Ⅱ Pre-reading Sample Answers

查看示例
  1. I once grew vegetables at school. They grew slowly, but we were able to harvest a small amount.
    我曾在学校种过蔬菜，长得比较慢，但最后收获了一点。
  2. I have visited an agricultural area. It had large fields, fewer buildings, and many farms.
    我去过农业地区，那里田地很大、建筑较少，也有很多农场。

Ⅲ Comprehension Check Questions

1
What danger has come from the use of antibiotics in factory farming?
Answer: Some bacteria can no longer be killed by antibiotics. These super-bacteria are causing health problems for humans.
中文：部分细菌产生了抗药性，抗生素无法杀死它们；这些“超级细菌”会危害人类健康。
2
What makes intensive farming different from traditional farming?
Answer: Intensive farming aims to produce the greatest amount of food in a limited area and relies heavily on chemicals, machines, and other technology.
中文：集约化农业追求单位面积的最高产量，并大量依赖化学品、机器和其他技术。
3
How was corn developed into the corn that we have today?
Answer: For hundreds or thousands of years, people selected and planted seeds from plants that looked better, tasted better, or produced more food.
中文：人们经过数百甚至数千年，选择外观更好、味道更好或产量更高的植物种子继续种植。
4
Why do modern farms use “Roundup”?
Answer: They use it to kill weeds. GMO crops are changed so that Roundup does not damage the crop plants.
中文：用它杀死杂草；转基因作物经过改造，不会被 Roundup 伤害。
5
What is necessary for GMO food to be sold in Japan?
Answer: It must be labeled as GMO food.
中文：必须标注为转基因食品。
6
What are two advantages of indoor farming?
Answer: It does not depend on the weather, and it does not need insect-killing chemicals because there are no insects indoors.
中文：不受天气影响；室内没有昆虫，因此不需要杀虫化学品。
7
Modern farming damages the land. What does organic farming do to the land?
Answer: It allows the land to continue producing food year after year and avoids creating salty, dead land.
中文：它能让土地年复一年继续生产食物，避免土壤盐化并变成无法耕种的死地。
8
What is one disadvantage of organic farming?
Answer: It requires more labor and other costs, so organic food is more expensive to produce.
中文：需要更多人工和其他成本，因此生产价格更高。
9
Why is it necessary to consider different methods of farming?
Answer: The population is growing, so we need more food, but each method has benefits and problems. We must feed everyone while protecting the environment.
中文：人口增长会增加粮食需求，但每种农业方式都有优缺点；必须兼顾供养人口与保护环境。
10
What is the main topic of this essay?
Answer: The essay compares modern farming methods—intensive farming and GMOs—with alternatives such as indoor and organic farming.
中文：文章比较了集约化农业、转基因农业，以及室内农业和有机农业等替代方法。
教材选择题答案（补充复习）
  1. A — Farm machinery
  2. B — Intensive farming involves chemicals.
  3. C — Roundup does not mean no other chemicals will ever be needed.
  4. B — GMOs are controlled by companies.
  5. C — People slowly changed grass into edible corn.
  6. B — The potato example shows insects being poisoned by GMO plants.
  7. A — Pure air and water are not stated as a feature.
  8. A — Crop rotation is used.
  9. A — Organic farming needs more human labor.
  10. A — Find the best way to produce more food.

Peace & Values 2.7 · Leaders of Social Change

练习纸中的 “Nobel Women’s Institute” 是拼写差异；教材正文使用 “Nobel Women’s Initiative”。

Ⅱ Comprehension Questions

1
What happened to Rigoberta Menchu’s brother and mother?
Answer: They were taken and killed by the Guatemalan army.
中文：他们被危地马拉军队抓走并杀害。
2
What did she do after she escaped to Mexico?
Answer: She risked her life to speak out against the Guatemalan army and organized groups to oppose oppression against indigenous people.
中文：她冒着生命危险公开反对军队，并组织团体反抗对原住民的压迫。
3
Why did she win a Nobel Peace Prize?
Answer: She worked to end oppression against indigenous people in Guatemala.
中文：她致力于结束危地马拉原住民遭受的压迫。
4
How did she help people in poverty?
Answer: She worked with medical companies to provide low-cost medicines.
中文：她与医药公司合作，提供低价药物。
5
What is the Nobel Women’s Initiative?
Answer: It is an organization that pushes for peace and equality around the world.
中文：这是一个推动全球和平与平等的组织。
6
What type of discrimination was common in the U.S. before the 1960s?
Answer: Racial segregation and discrimination against Black people were common in restaurants, schools, buses, and public facilities.
中文：当时普遍存在种族隔离和针对黑人的歧视，涉及餐厅、学校、公交车和公共设施。
7
What is one example of nonviolent resistance?
Answer: The bus boycott organized by Martin Luther King, Jr. is one example.
中文：马丁·路德·金组织的公交车抵制运动。
8
Before the bus boycott, what did a Black person have to do if a white person was standing?
Answer: The Black passenger had to stand up and give the seat to the white person.
中文：黑人乘客必须起身把座位让给白人。
9
How did Black people protest the law about behavior on buses?
Answer: They stopped riding the buses and often walked many miles to work and back.
中文：他们停止乘坐公交车，甚至步行很远上下班。
10
What was the result of this protest?
Answer: The government decided that separate seats for white and Black people were against the law.
中文：政府最终裁定公交车上的种族隔离座位违法。
11
What did the Taliban ban?
Answer: They banned girls from going to school.
中文：他们禁止女孩上学。
12
What did Malala Yousafzai do when she was still a child?
Answer: She spoke out publicly against the ban on girls’ education.
中文：她公开反对禁止女孩受教育的规定。
13
How did the Taliban react when Malala spoke out?
Answer: They decided she should die, and a gunman shot her in the head on a bus.
中文：他们决定杀死她，一名枪手在公交车上朝她头部开枪。
14
How did Malala Yousafzai’s life change?
Answer: She survived, received medical treatment in England, gained worldwide support, and later won the Nobel Peace Prize.
中文：她幸存下来并在英国接受治疗，获得全球支持，之后赢得诺贝尔和平奖。
15
What does Malala Yousafzai do now?
Answer: She continues to speak against religious extremism and works through the Malala Fund to give girls equal educational opportunities.
中文：她继续反对宗教极端主义，并通过马拉拉基金帮助女孩获得平等教育机会。
16
What was Mahatma Gandhi’s life like as a child and young man?
Answer: He was born into a good family, married at thirteen, and received a good education in India and England, where he studied philosophy and religion.
中文：他出身良好，13岁结婚，在印度和英国接受良好教育，并学习哲学和宗教。
17
What did Mahatma Gandhi fight against in South Africa?
Answer: He fought against apartheid and racial segregation.
中文：他反对种族隔离制度。
18
What did Mahatma Gandhi do when he returned to India?
Answer: He became a leader of nonviolent protests against British rule.
中文：他成为反对英国统治的非暴力抗议领袖。
19
What is one of the actions that Mahatma Gandhi led in India?
Answer: He led a 250-mile Salt March to the sea to protest the British salt tax.
中文：他领导人们步行250英里到海边取盐，以抗议英国的盐税。
20
What is the type of protest he used called today?
Answer: It is called nonviolent protest or nonviolent resistance.
中文：这种方式称为非暴力抗议或非暴力抵抗。
